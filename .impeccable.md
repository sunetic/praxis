## Design Context

### Users

Praxis is used by database administrators and developers who investigate live database behavior, gather evidence, and carry out operational workflows through an AI agent. They need to follow long-running work without losing the conversation or being overwhelmed by implementation detail.

### Brand Personality

Professional, restrained, and responsive. The interface should create confidence through clear progress, evidence, and predictable controls while remaining calm during long-running operations.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light, compact technical workspace inspired by the interaction restraint of Codex and Claude Code. Conversation is the primary surface. Runtime state stays close to the current assistant turn, and raw tool payloads remain available through progressive disclosure rather than dominating the viewport. Avoid card-heavy status banners, decorative AI effects, and screen-filling diagnostic output by default.

### Design Principles

1. Show continuous progress without competing with the answer.
2. Stream real model output as it arrives; do not simulate responsiveness with a cosmetic typewriter effect.
3. Keep tool calls collapsed and summarized by default, expanding automatically only when user action or error recovery requires attention.
4. Preserve evidence and advanced detail on demand through assistant-ui primitives.
5. Maintain accessible motion, clear status semantics, and bilingual product copy.
